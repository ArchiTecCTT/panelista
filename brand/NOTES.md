# Panelista identity
Three people share one table, making the evaluator panel the central symbol.
An open check in the table signals a completed, constructive review.
The amber middle head gives the group a warm focal point without ranking its members.
Deep green grounds the identity in trust, with white button text meeting WCAG AA.
Coral faces and amber ratings add warmth; pale green-white gives layouts room to breathe.
The wordmark uses original path-drawn letters with rounded terminals and open spacing.
Illustrations show students and volunteers working together, with varied skin tones and hairstyles.
Use the simplified favicon at 16 to 32 px and inline currentColor artwork for monochrome contexts.
Keep the wordmark on a light plate over dark backgrounds and label verification badges in the interface.
